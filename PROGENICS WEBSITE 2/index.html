Frequently Asked Questions

Is genetic testing accurate and reliable?
+
Yes! Our testing uses advanced next-generation sequencing (NGS) with 99.9% accuracy. All tests are conducted in ISO-certified laboratories following international quality standards. Results are verified by experienced analysts.
How long does it take to get my results?
+
Results are typically available within 2-3 weeks (based on the type of test choosen) after we receive your sample. You'll receive a comprehensive report and can schedule a free consultation with our counselors to understand your results.
What sample do I need to provide?
+
We provide a simple at-home collection kit. Most tests require either a saliva sample or a blood sample (via a painless finger prick). Just follow the instructions and mail back your sample.
Is my genetic data secure and private?
+
Absolutely. We are HIPAA compliant with military-grade encryption. Your genetic data is never shared with third parties without explicit consent. You have full control over your data and can request deletion at any time.
Can I share my results with my doctor?
+
Yes! Your results are in a clinical-grade format that you can share with your physician. We recommend discussing your results with your doctor to understand how to apply the personalized recommendations to your health plan.
Do I need my doctor's approval to order?
+
No, you do not need a doctor's approval to order our predictive and wellness genetic tests. They are completely direct-to-consumer.
