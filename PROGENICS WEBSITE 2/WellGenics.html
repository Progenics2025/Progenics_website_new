Frequently Asked Questions

What is WellGenics and how is it different from generic health tests?
WellGenics analyzes the specific genetic markers that control your nutrition metabolism, exercise response, supplement absorption, and lifestyle predispositions. Unlike generic health tests, our recommendations are built exclusively on YOUR DNA not population averages.
How does my DNA tell me what diet to follow?
Your genes control how quickly you metabolize carbohydrates, fats, and proteins. Some people gain weight easily on high-carb diets and others can't absorb vitamin D efficiently because of their genetics. WellGenics maps these genetic factors to give you a precision nutrition plan that actually works for your body.
Can WellGenics help if I've tried many diets and failed?
Absolutely. Most diet failures happen because people follow generic plans that don't account for their genetics. WellGenics reveals exactly why previous diets failed and prescribes an approach matched to how your body actually works.
What exercise insights will WellGenics give me?
You'll discover your genetic muscle fiber composition (power vs endurance), VO2 max potential, injury risk factors, and optimal recovery time. This means you'll know whether you're built for sprinting or marathons and train accordingly to see maximum results.
Will WellGenics tell me which supplements I need?
Yes. WellGenics identifies deficiencies in your absorption capacity for Vitamin D, B12, Iron, Omega-3, and more. Instead of buying random supplements, you get targeted recommendations for exactly what YOUR body needs.
How long until I see results following my WellGenics plan?
Most customers see noticeable changes within 3–4 weeks of following their personalized plan. Since the plan is built on your DNA, it works more efficiently than generic wellness programs.
Is the test safe and is my data private?
Yes. Your saliva sample collection is completely safe, and all genetic data is encrypted and HIPAA-compliant. Your genetic information is never shared with insurance companies, employers, or third parties without your explicit consent.
Do I need to come to a lab or hospital?
No. The entire process is done from the comfort of your home. We send you a kit, you take a simple saliva swab, and send it back in the prepaid envelope. Results are delivered online within weeks time.
