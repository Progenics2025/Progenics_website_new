Introduction

Respecting your privacy is important to Progenics Laboratories Private Limited. The purpose of this statement is to inform you of the kinds of information that we may gather when you use our website, how we may use the information, and how we may disclose it to other parties.

By using our products and services, you agree that this Privacy Statement is acceptable to you and that Progenics Laboratories is permitted to use your personal information as stated below.

Information We Collect

Visitors to our website do not provide any personal information. However, our server might automatically collect some data such as your IP address, browser type, and duration of your visit.

If you choose to contact us or subscribe to our newsletter, you may provide your name, email address, and other contact information.

Customers who purchase genetic tests provide their health history through a questionnaire. This information is stored in our secure Laboratory Information Management System.

How We Use Cookies

A cookie is a small piece of data that is sent to your browser from a web server and stored on your computer’s hard drive. Progenics Laboratories uses cookies to collect information on how you use our website, such as which pages you visit and for how long.

Most web browsers are set to accept cookies by default. You can choose to set your browser to remove or reject cookies. Please note that if you choose to remove or reject cookies, this could affect the availability and functionality of our Services.

Health & Genetic Information

The health history and genetic data that we collect from our customers is used solely for the purpose of providing the requested genetic tests or counseling services.

This information is stored in our secure system and is only accessible by authorized personnel. We do not share this information with third parties without your explicit consent, except as required by law.

Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously and have implemented measures to protect it from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. We use secure servers and encryption to protect your data.
