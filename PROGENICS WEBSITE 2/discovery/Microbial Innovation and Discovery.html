MIND's Mission: Advancing Microbiome Science for Healthcare

The human microbiome is one of medicine's final frontiers. Trillions of microorganisms influence immunity, metabolism, mental health, disease susceptibility, and treatment response. Understanding microbiome composition and function is transforming how we diagnose and treat disease.

MIND exists to accelerate microbiome science from laboratory discovery to clinical reality. We conduct research, develop diagnostics, advance therapeutics, and partner with institutions worldwide to unlock the microbiome's potential.
