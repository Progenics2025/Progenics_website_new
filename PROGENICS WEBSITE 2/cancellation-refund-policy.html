Cancellation & Refund

Smooth Support Process

Policy Overview

Progenics Laboratories values its customers and is committed to providing excellent service. This statement outlines our policies for order cancellations and refunds.

  • Orders can be canceled before the kit is dispatched.
  • Refunds are issued if we are unable to provide the test for any reason.
  • Damaged kits are eligible for replacement or full refund.
STEP 01

Request

Email connect@progenicslabs.com with your order details and reason.

STEP 02

Review

Our team reviews your request within 5 business days for approval.

STEP 03

Processing

Approved refunds are credited back to your original payment within 7-10 days.

Non-Refundable Terms

Please note that refunds will not be issued if the sample kit has already been dispatched, if the provided sample is of poor quality, or if the customer fails to provide a sample within the specified timeframe.
