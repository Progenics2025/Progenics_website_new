Logistics & Shipping

Secure Sample Transport

Kit Dispatch

Once you book a test, a specialized sample collection kit is dispatched within 24-48 hours. Our kits are designed for maximum integrity during transit.

Sample Collection

Follow the detailed instructions included in your kit to collect your sample comfortably at home. No clinic visit is required.

Secure Pickup

Schedule a pickup through our portal. Our logistics partners will collect the sample and ensure it is transported under controlled conditions.

Lab Arrival

Samples arrive at our Hyderabad laboratory within 24-72 hours. You'll receive a notification as soon as the analysis begins.

Triple-Layer Packaging

We use IATA-compliant triple-layer packaging to ensure biological samples remain stable and secure regardless of the distance.

Real-time Tracking

Monitor your sample's journey every step of the way with our advanced real-time tracking system via SMS and Email updates.
